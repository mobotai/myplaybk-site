Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how myPlaybk ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use the myPlaybk iOS app ("the App"). We built myPlaybk to help families document and organize what matters — not to collect or profit from your data.

1. What myPlaybk Is

myPlaybk is a personal household documentation app. It helps you and your family record, organize, and reference important information — accounts, insurance, property, contacts, and more.

2. Data You Enter

All content you create in myPlaybk — entries, notes, dates, person tags, category names, and attached files — is your data. We do not access, view, or process this content except as described below.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

On Your Device
Your myPlaybk data is stored locally on your device using Apple's CoreData framework.

iCloud Sync (CloudKit)
If you are signed into iCloud, your data syncs across your Apple devices using Apple CloudKit. This means your data is stored in your personal iCloud account — on Apple's infrastructure, not ours. Apple's privacy policy governs this storage. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Attached Files
Photos and documents you attach to entries are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage directory. These files are included in your standard iCloud device backup but are not independently synced across devices at this time.

4. AI Features (Ask myPlaybk)

myPlaybk includes an AI assistant feature ("Ask myPlaybk") powered by Anthropic's Claude AI.

When you use Ask myPlaybk:

  • The text of your question and relevant entry data from your myPlaybk are sent to our secure proxy server and forwarded to Anthropic's API.
  • Anthropic processes this data to generate a response.
  • We do not permanently store your queries or Anthropic's responses on our servers.
  • Anthropic's data handling is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

We recommend not storing usernames or passwords in myPlaybk. The app is designed to document what you have and who manages it — not to store credentials. We also recommend not including highly sensitive information (Social Security numbers, full account numbers, passwords) in your AI queries.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Your name, email address, or account credentials
  • Financial account numbers or passwords
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Analytics or usage tracking data
  • Your location

6. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Their Privacy Policy
Apple CloudKit iCloud sync apple.com/privacy
Anthropic Claude AI assistant anthropic.com/privacy

No other third-party services receive your data.

7. Children's Privacy

myPlaybk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your myPlaybk data lives in your iCloud account and on your devices. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual entries or categories within the app
  • Delete the app from your device to remove locally stored data and attachments
  • Manage or delete your iCloud data through your device's iCloud settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
